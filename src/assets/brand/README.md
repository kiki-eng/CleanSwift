# CleanSwift logo

`cleanswift-icon.png` is the opaque 1024 × 1024 master used by the shared
`BrandLogo` component. Native iOS and Android launcher assets are resized copies.
Keep the master square; native launchers apply their own masks.

Created with the built-in image generation tool.

Design prompt: Create a polished app icon for CleanSwift, a trusted home cleaning
marketplace. A bold white C-shaped sweeping ribbon suggests swift motion and a
clean home, with a small four-point mint sparkle at its upper-right opening.
Use a full-bleed teal #0D9488 background, a crisp vector-like silhouette, and
generous safe margins. No text, border, watermark, or rounded outer corners.

Final refinement prompt: Preserve the C sweep and mint sparkle silhouette. Place
it on a fully opaque solid teal #0D9488 background covering the entire square.
Flatten the symbol to white and mint with crisp edges, removing bevels, glows,
textures, and shadows. Keep the symbol within the central 60% of the square.
No text or transparency.

Native launch screens also use the logo: iOS `LaunchLogo.imageset` and
`LaunchScreen.storyboard`; Android `LaunchTheme` uses a centered logo before
Android 12 and the system splash icon on Android 12 and later.
